PRIVACY & PROJECT FILES

Know where
your information goes.

This is a website design preview for Blue Star. The project form connects to the existing Blue Star intake service.

Project submissions

The embedded form sends your contact details, project information and selected files to Blue Star’s existing hosted service. GitHub Pages serves the preview pages; it is not the storage service for your project files.

The review workflow may process project details and files through ChatGPT/OpenAI to prepare a preliminary Blue Star brief for Josh. The brief may be emailed to Josh through Gmail from the connected sending account. Josh checks the information and makes the final recommendations. Delivery is not guaranteed; call about urgent deadlines.

Only submit material you are authorized to share. Avoid unnecessary personal information, passwords or access codes. The form explains its current review consent before you send.

Josh’s review

Josh reviews the project and makes the final decisions about the recommended approach, scope, quantities, materials, price and schedule. An upload is not a firm quote, booking or construction instruction.

Privacy details and requests

Read the intake service’s privacy and file information for the full current handling details. For questions about a submission, contact Joshua@bluestarsprayfoam.com or (864) 373-4464.

This preview

The preview stores only your light/dark color preference in your browser. It does not add a marketing analytics or advertising tracker. GitHub and the intake host may process technical information needed to provide their services.
