COMMERCIAL QUICK ANSWERS

Before you send.

Can I send a bid invitation or scope sheet?+

Yes. Include it with the current drawings and any specifications or addenda. Put the due date and contact person in the description. Sending a package does not by itself confirm bid participation.

Can Blue Star suggest a different insulation approach?+

Josh can identify questions or possible alternatives. Any change to a specified system must go through the project’s required design and approval process.

Can I get an immediate budget from the website?+

The website does not issue an automatic price. Josh reviews the information first so a discussion about budget is tied to a defined scope and visible assumptions.

Do I need a complete drawing package to begin?+

No. A description, photos or rough surface area can start an early discussion. The information needed for a formal bid will be confirmed separately.
